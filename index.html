Kerbside

Takings, costs and tax, sorted on the rank.

A money and tax app for UK taxi and private hire drivers. This clickable prototype is filled with sample data for a London PHV driver.

Try it

  • Start a shift on Today, then end it to log your miles.
  • Tap the amber + and add a fare on the meter keypad.
  • Missed a few days? Pick Date range in the add sheet, or tap "Nothing logged" under Needs attention.
  • Open Tax and review the Making Tax Digital quarterly update.
  • Check licence and insurance reminders under Vehicle.

Tax figures use 2026/27 rates. Nothing is sent to HMRC, DVLA or anyone else. Your changes stay in this browser.
